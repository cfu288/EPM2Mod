## Notes on non-CORRECT claims

**12-35 (WRONG).** The sentinel scheme described (0x9B00 user / 0x6400 max / 0x9C00 min, clamped to the record's [+14, +16]) belongs only to 0x7E694412, and only 0x7E6940C6 calls it. 0x7E694181 and 0x7E6941DA use two different helpers with different sentinels (0x6300 appears in both, and 0x9C00 in neither), different fallbacks (fields +20/+22, defaults 1536/3328; 0x7E6A3174 when byte +40 = 5) and fixed clamp ranges ([1536, 3328] and [−1536, 3072]), not the record's [min, max]. 0x7E6941DA then clamps its third result between its first two results. The "art codes 69–181" case list is {3, 4, 69–74, 85, 86, 91, 96, 145, 163, 181}, not a range, and it includes P and A. That these are art codes comes from §1.3; it is not shown here.

**12-39 (MINOR).** The calls and the 256 / 128 / other split are right. Tv has a fourth path (value at the upper limit → 0x7E6A8F11), and the Av variants have a computed fallback. One table is at 0x7E7E0DFE, outside 0x7E7E1xxx. The decoded tables (shutter rationals in 1, 1/2 and 1/3 EV steps; F-number × 10; bias tenths) support the Tv/Av/bias labels, which the map gave without decoding the tables.

**12-78 (MINOR).** The listed fields hold, except that `gyako` does not come from the +60 block. It is the byte at capture block +336, passed as an argument. The three offsets belong to face_num, mode and bv_std.

**12-187 (MINOR).** The printed field list is right, but the function is not a dump routine. 0x7E5E6063 computes the spot AF areas and prints them only when debug bit mask 0x2000 is set at 0x6054C7A4.

**12-213 (MINOR).** The mechanism is described correctly. The address bounds are not. The lists start at 0x7E8503FC and run to 0x7E851663. The templates end at 0x7E8503FB. As written, the two ranges overlap.

**12-249 (MINOR).** Every constant checks out, but CFAPattern (Exif 0xA302) and ValidBits (ImageProcessing 0x0611) are listed under the heading "ORF IFD0 values". The "12-bit in 16-bit containers" conclusion is inferred, not verified.
