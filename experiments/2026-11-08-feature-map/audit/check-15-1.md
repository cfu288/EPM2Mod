## Notes on the non-CORRECT claims

**15a-59 (MINOR).** The factory and the layout are verified. Two counts are wrong. (1) The class has 28 derived classes, not 29: the count of 29 adds the factory 0x7EDC7ECB to the callers of 0x7EDC7EAA. (2) The factory serves 111 list ids in the registry 0x7EFE180C–0x7EFE21CB. The number 110 is the count of TSV page(list) rows, that is, ids that openPage can reach. List 110 has a registry row with this factory, but page 110 is the object page 0x7EDE1E3D, and no direct caller of getListPage passes 110. Suggested text: "factory 0x7EDC7ECB: 111 registry ids (110 reachable from openPage; list 110 is not); 28 derived classes".

**15a-133 (MINOR).** The page-to-getter mapping, the 168-B size, panel 4, the vt+44 call and the (2,4)/(4,2) arguments are all verified. The name "PENPAL album copy" is not verified. The earlier review marked it as inferred and said the media codes are unproven, but the section places it in a "V unless marked" table with no I mark. From the name-id table, the call is `MediaCtrl::transferImageAll(src, dst, cb)`. Suggested text: "P2S / S2P all-image transfer (MediaCtrl vt+44 = transferImageAll with (2,4) / (4,2)); I: PENPAL album ↔ card".
