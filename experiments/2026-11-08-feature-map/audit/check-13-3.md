## Notes on the non-CORRECT claims

**13e-12.** The engine (task entry 0x7E58204D, from the task table), the helpers 0x7E5823CC–0x7E5825DB, the 27 × 20 B state table and the name pointer are all correct. The doc writes inclusive ends elsewhere (mcon 0x7E6FCA60–0x7E70787F, light 0x7E5E2258–0x7E5E2418). By that rule the end here must be 0x7E582601. 0x7E582602 is the `movm` that starts the take debug-command function. This is a trivial off-by-one error.

**13e-15.** The availability function 0x7E5E2258 and F011 0x7E5E22A6 behave as §2.4 says, and the two helpers 0x7E5E1B11 and 0x7E5DEBAD are correct. But the range also covers the four light-exec callbacks 0x7E5E2300–0x7E5E2418, and §2.4 itself says they are not selection code. Also, the callback 0x7E5E23F5 is loaded by 0x7E5E2387, not by strobe_light_exec. Side finding outside this sample, at §2.4 line 156: 0x7E5E1B11 returns 0x2C50 when 0x7E509CA3(0x1215) **== 1** (`mov 11344,d0; cmp 1,d1; beq exit`), not "≠ 1". It returns 0 early when 0x7E5E1CAF() = 1 and 0x7E457C75(0x2981) = 1.

**13e-236.** Every sub-assertion is verified: the only callers, camera_end, the veneer, the HFW method, the notice value 0x03010007, the strobe API calls and their order, and the other caller 0x7E701E42 through 0x7E701D68. But the 05 sentence is wrong only in its module label. The call edge 0x7E701D27 → 0x7E68CCBD → SERV_S1 is real. The bold "**wrong**" reads as if the call path were false. Better wording: "0x7E701D27 is mcon code, not REC".

**13f-81.** The register set, the SRAM toggling of 0x10, and the users 0x7E95EFC8 and 0x7EEC6822 are confirmed. The `aud_tool_ac3` name for 0x7E95EFC8 is inferred from neighbouring functions; no strings support it, but the claim is labelled V. The twin accessor 0x7E95F008, for the second unit, is missing from the list. Its callers are image-engine and CPU1 functions (0x7E6D80B3 is the IRQ 101/104/109 attach site), so a non-audio client also uses these units.

**13f-82.** The channel order, the ROM table and IRQs 77–90 are exact. The driver-function range stops at the shell 0x7E469BD1. It leaves out the init entry and the client-facing request/transfer functions at 0x7E469C16–0x7E469D75, which the §2.3 clients call.

**13f-88.** The IRQs, the ISRs and wdt_set_time are confirmed. The range runs into GIO accessor code at 0x7E45D9C8–0x7E45DA28. "Always-on domain" is an interpretation, made from the backup, RTC, power-on factor and watchdog registers, but it sits under a V label. §2.6 itself marks those register names as I.

**13f-230.** The runner itself is described correctly: the ring at 0x60545454, 32 entries of 12 bytes, the `{fn, arg, u16 d0, u16 kind}` layout, the meaning of kinds 0/1/2, and the three post helpers. But the task blocks on an event flag (OlyEvWt), not on a queue. The thunk series 0x7E5C4EBC… belongs to the FILECLBK task (command class 0x1, called synchronously from 0x7E5C50DE), so they are not FILEFGND jobs. Also, the thunks do not share one `{ret, a0, d0, d1, a1}` layout.
