# Patch history

Superseded, experimental and failed patch specs from the film-look work, kept as a record of what
was flashed (see `docs/camera-log.md`) and of the steps in
`experiments/2026-11-09-film-portra/NOTES.md`. They are not part of the current build.

Do not flash these without reading the camera log first. In particular:

- `slots7-*`, `remap7-code`, `remap7-neutral`: live view freezes in Soft Focus, Pin Hole, Diorama.
- `slots12-*`, `film-pro400h-pa2-s12`: never flashed; uses effect-filter numbers that are known to
  freeze live view.

Several specs overlap (each film patch was written to stand alone in its slot at the time), so they
cannot be combined freely. The current build's list is in the top-level `README.md`.
