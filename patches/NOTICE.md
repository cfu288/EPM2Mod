The film look specs in this folder and in `history/` (`seven-films-*`, `film-*`, `check-portra-in-new-slots`,
`remap7-neutral` and similar) hold matrices and curves derived from spektrafilm by Andrea Volpato,
https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, modified by fitting them to the
Olympus E-PM2. The `expect` fields hold original Olympus firmware bytes. See [`../NOTICE.md`](../NOTICE.md).
